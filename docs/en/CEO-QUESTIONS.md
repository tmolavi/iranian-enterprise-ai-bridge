# 100 Strategic CEO Benchmark Questions Catalog
## Validating Enterprise AI Readiness on Iranian Business & Operational Realities

---

## Benchmark Question Clusters

### 1. Finance, Revenue & Costing (Q01–Q15)
- **Q01:** Why did net sales decrease this month compared to last month?
- **Q02:** What is the gross profit and gross margin broken down by product line?
- **Q03:** What is the variance between actual COGS and the approved operating budget?
- **Q04:** Which cost centers experienced the highest negative budget variance in Q2?
- **Q05:** What was total operating revenue in H1 compared to the same period last year?

### 2. Treasury, Cash Flow & Receivables (Q16–Q30)
- **Q11:** What is our current liquid cash and bank position versus committed liabilities this month?
- **Q12:** What is our Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and the status of 60+ days overdue receivables?
- **Q13:** Which customers exceeded their credit limits yet continue placing orders?
- **Q14:** How many commercial promissory notes or checks bounced this month?
- **Q15:** What is the projected cash deficit on the 28th of the month for payroll and tax liabilities?

### 3. Manufacturing, OEE & Shop Floor (Q31–Q50)
- **Q21:** What was plant OEE last month and where is the primary production bottleneck?
- **Q22:** Which production lines exceeded scrap thresholds and what is the financial cost impact?
- **Q23:** What is the attainment rate of the board-approved production plan this quarter?
- **Q24:** How many unplanned downtime hours occurred and what were the primary root causes?
- **Q25:** What are the MTBF and MTTR metrics for critical rolling and furnace equipment?

### 4. Inventory & Supply Chain (Q51–Q75)
- **Q31:** What is the value of dead/slow-moving inventory with zero movement for 6+ months?
- **Q32:** Which strategic raw materials dropped below minimum safety stock levels?
- **Q33:** What is our inventory turnover ratio and average days sales of inventory (DSI)?

### 5. Governance, Contracts & Risk (Q76–Q100)
- **Q51:** What is the completion status of resolutions and action items from the last board meeting?
- **Q52:** Which major customer or supplier contracts expire in the next 30 days?
